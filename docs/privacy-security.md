# Privacy and security

**Scope.** Udyam Digital uses fictional business proofs, mock registries and simulated payment and installation. Use synthetic data. The consent screen explains that the evidence is used to verify the current demo order. Owners can withdraw before payment. Withdrawal closes the case and removes its verification result and slot hold.

**Browser-local OCR.** Keeping proof images on the device is a deliberate privacy choice. The browser de-skews or rejects poor-quality scans and runs self-hosted Tesseract WASM. Verification requests contain extracted fields for the required proofs. The server repeats GSTIN checksum and registry checks, matches document and registry names and addresses, validates dates and flags conflicting state codes or a near registry match. Browser OCR scores and fields are untrusted inputs. The checks establish consistency with the mock records. A production verifier would need trusted evidence or an approved identity service to establish authenticity.

**Storage and access.** The case store keeps a consent receipt, quote and order state, masked evidence outcomes and audit events. Raw proof fields are evaluated in memory. The GSTIN captured for eligibility is represented in the case by a masked reference and server-side hash. Imported sample images require the authorized case and recorded consent. Case bearers and one-time resume codes are hashed on the server. Resume codes expire in 10 minutes and have an attempt limit. Inactive cases expire after 24 hours and are pruned when new cases are created. Operations uses case sessions already available in the current browser and simulates staff roles.

**External services.** With a model configured, the advisor sends the needs message after masking common tax identifiers, phone numbers and email addresses. Proofs and registry results stay out of model requests. Provider keys are server configuration. WhatsApp verifies the raw-body signature and account phone ID, keeps keyed sender and message digests for case routing and deduplication, and sends the reply through Meta. The recipient’s number is used in memory for delivery. The browser receives the handoff code in a URL fragment.

**Production controls.** Before use with real subscribers, add role-based staff access, an enforced deletion schedule, managed secrets and rate limits, approved registry and identity integrations, and a legal review of notice, consent and retention.
